Straight answers

BUILT ON REAL DATA.

Can I analyse a real racing video?

The intended product will accept onboard footage and identify visible driving events. Reliable speed, brake pressure and throttle values require synced GPS or vehicle telemetry. Video alone cannot provide those measurements accurately.

Will the AI talk to me while I race?

That requires a Windows companion application to read supported simulator telemetry, plus a low-latency voice service. The website preview demonstrates the product direction, not a working live coach.

Which simulators will be supported?

Support must be added simulator by simulator using their telemetry interfaces. A sensible first target would be Assetto Corsa and Assetto Corsa Competizione, followed by iRacing and other titles.

Can I subscribe now?

Not yet. The pricing cards are illustrative. A secure subscription system needs a backend, Stripe account, checkout session, webhook verification and customer portal.
